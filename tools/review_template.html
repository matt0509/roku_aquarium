Palette and drawing rules

Every piece is drawn from this palette. No color goes above 92% brightness (no pure white), which keeps highlights soft on an OLED.

    {{SWATCHES}}
  • Storybook illustration: clean shapes, a deep indigo outline (not black) and simple three-tone shading.
  • Light comes from the water surface above, so tops are lighter and undersides darker.
  • Natural anatomy, simplified. No smiles or cartoon faces.
  • Some sprites are drawn in grays and recolored on the Roku, which gives many color varieties from one drawing.

Mockup views are still images assembled from the samples. In the screensaver everything moves: fish swim, plants sway and the camera drifts very slowly.
